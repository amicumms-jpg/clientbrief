📖 How ClientBrief Works

1. Create a Brief: Select the information you need from your client (company details, brand colors, asset folders) and enter your WhatsApp number.

2. Share the Link: Generate a clean, custom link instantly and send it to your client via email, chat, or DM.

3. Client Fills It Out: Your client opens the link on mobile or desktop, fills in the requested fields, and attaches public folder links.

4. Direct to WhatsApp: With one click, all details are neatly formatted and sent straight into your WhatsApp chat. No more chasing!
